+## Tristram atmosphere

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative parallax background for the independent Diablo 3D development website. Using the supplied UI reference board ONLY as art direction, create a standalone atmospheric matte painting of a gothic medieval village inspired by Tristram, no user interface, no lettering, no logos, no borders. It is explicitly illustrative background art for the website, NOT a real screenshot or evidence of implemented gameplay. View from elevated three-quarter angle across dark timber and stone houses and distant cathedral, rough paths, bare trees, restrained warm amber window/candle lights. Muted near-black charcoal and desaturated brown stone; antique gold glow, smoky deep atmosphere; retain tactile fine detail without bright colors. Landscape extremely wide 2400x1200 or comparable. Main buildings along far left and far right and lower third, center upper-middle area quiet dark negative space for white/gold large serif text placed later in HTML. Strong composition close to the existing reference's homepage and roadmap section background. Elegantly gloomy rather than horror gore, modest subtle warm light. No characters in foreground, no interface, no text, no watermarks, no simulated screenshots, no screens. Purpose is a clearly labelled decorative atmospheric illustration.

## Journal atmosphere

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative parallax background for the Diablo 3D development journal website; artistic illustration, not gameplay evidence. Match the dark warm antique editorial atmosphere of the supplied UI reference board. Standalone wide background matte painting, no interface, text, logos or border. A medieval working table at night, an open aged notebook, a few closed books, a single wax candle with restrained warm amber light, a small model-like timber roof silhouette and indistinct architectural sketches WITHOUT readable lettering, shadowy stone room and a narrow view of bare trees outside. Main still-life concentrated on far right third and lower-right, left two thirds dark quiet negative space for white serif heading and content in HTML. Charcoal-black, desaturated brown, ivory paper and antique gold accents; dignified, subtle, cinematic rich texture. Landscape 2400x1200 or comparable. It is a plainly labelled ambient illustration for a developer's diary, never a fake screenshot. No bright orange oversaturation, visual clutter, fantasy characters, weapons, user data or readable documents. Similar composition to the DEVLOG panel's books and candle in reference board.
